About

Good architecture should feel calm, work hard and age well. I aim for spaces that are open, flexible and honest about how people will use them.

My experience spans governmental and private projects: residential and institutional buildings, an art center, commercial work, and residential exteriors and interiors. I lead planning, design and visualization, prepare drawing sets that meet municipal and national standards, and coordinate with clients, contractors and multidisciplinary teams on site so the design intent is built as drawn.
