About

Hi, I’m Bigyan, an architect based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I design modern, minimal and open-concept spaces: functional, timeless environments that balance aesthetics with practicality.

My experience spans governmental and private projects: residential and institutional buildings, an art center, commercial work, landscapes, and residential exteriors and interiors. I lead planning, design and visualization, prepare drawing sets that meet municipal and national standards, and coordinate with clients, contractors and multidisciplinary teams on site so the design intent is built as drawn.
